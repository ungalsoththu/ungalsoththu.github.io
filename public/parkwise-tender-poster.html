THE INVITATION-ONLY TENDER சொத்து உங்களுடையது — அழைப்பு யாருக்கு?

Chennai Unified Metropolitan Transport Authority is buying the software brain for citywide “Edge AI Smart Parking and Violation Enforcement.” Only invited firms may bid. The tender does not publish its value, its invitee list, or its reason for shutting the door — while one vendor has already spent a year running a parking pilot wearing CUMTA’s own logo.

  • JUL 2024

    Moving Tech Innovations (Bengaluru, Juspay group) wins CUMTA’s ticketing tender CUMTA/9112/TICKETING/2024 → runs the Chennai One app. Evaluation records and contract terms remain unpublished. — tender record; TNIE, Jul 2024

  • 20 SEP 2025

    Chennai One launches. TN pays CUMTA a ~₹3 crore/yr platform fee (1.39% of tickets) to the vendor. — TNIE, 16 Sep 2025

  • 14 NOV 2025

  • 26–28 MAR 2026

ParkWise markets itself to cities as adoptable “without tenders.” We write to CUMTA asking for the authorising paper. No reply. — api.parkwise.city config; parkwise.city; letter dt. 09-Sep-2026

  • 12 NOV 2026

    CUMTA floats CUMTA/0072/2026-Edge AI — “Selection of System Integrator for Edge AI Smart Parking and Violation Enforcement System Software.” Open Limited: invited bidders only. The technical spec file is literally named VideoAnalytics.pdf.

  • 27 NOV 2026

    Bids close, 3:00 PM. Opening at 4:00 PM. The public still cannot name a single invited firm.

2The question

Is this tender written so that only one firm can win — the same firm that has been operating a CUMTA-branded parking pilot without any published contract?

We can’t answer that yet — because CUMTA has not published what would answer it. Here is exactly what the public record shows, and what it hides.

Signal on the recordStatus
Restricted “Open Limited” method — with no reason published anywhereCONFIRMED
Tender value shown as “NA” — so the 30-day window required above ₹10 crore cannot even be checked (15 days = legal floor only up to ₹10 crore)CONFIRMED
No pre-bid meeting, no clarification dates — competitors get no room to query the specCONFIRMED
Scope words “Violation Enforcement” + spec file named VideoAnalytics match ParkWise’s marketed enforcement stack (ANPR challans, Parivahan integration)INFERENCE
One vendor holds ~12 months of de-facto operational access under CUMTA branding — a head start no open process would createINFERENCE
Invitee list · RfP authorship · eligibility criteria inside the spec — the three documents that would settle the questionWITHHELD

Read this carefully: none of this proves rigging, and we allege none. A restricted tender is lawful when reasoned. What is proven is that this procurement cannot be audited from outside — value hidden, invitees hidden, reason hidden, spec unarchived. That is not a paperwork detail. That is the difference between a market and a hand-back.

3Five papers that end the question

  1. The estimate. Publish the tender value/estimate. It decides whether the 15-day window is legal at all.
  2. The invitee list + reason. Who was invited to bid on a public enforcement system, and under what recorded justification.
  3. The ParkWise papers. Every contract, MoU, pilot authorisation or work order with Moving Tech, Automicle or ParkWise — and who authorised the CUMTA logo upload to cumta.org.
  4. The spec’s authorship. Who wrote VideoAnalytics.pdf — in-house, a consultant, or a vendor draft.
  5. The award, in the open. TN Tenders Act §10(7) already obliges CUMTA to send the winner’s name and rejection reasons to the Tender Bulletin. We will be reading it.
