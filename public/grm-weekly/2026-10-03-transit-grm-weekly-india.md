## Method and limits

X searches were constrained to 26 September–3 October 2026 and focused on direct agency mentions/replies and actionable passenger service failures. Counts are distinct public complaint threads; follow-ups were merged. Suggestions, praise, official announcements and unrelated transit posts were excluded. An agency reply means any visible response, including a generic forwarding or docket; it does **not** imply resolution. Response intervals are approximate elapsed times decoded from X post IDs. This is an X-visible sample, not a count of all complaints received by the agencies. No hourly scheduler data was used.
