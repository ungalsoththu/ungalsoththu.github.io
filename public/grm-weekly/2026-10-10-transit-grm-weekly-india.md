# 🚌 Weekly India Transit Grievance Redressal Audit

**Window:** 3 October 2026, 09:00 IST – 10 October 2026, 09:00 IST  
**Scope:** The ten agencies in the configured watchlist; passenger grievances and visible agency replies on X. Searches used the configured current/history-handle patterns and agency search terms, then the required zero-result checks. X date filters are approximate; post-ID times were checked against the exact cutoff.  
**Result:** **47 distinct complaints; 20 received a visible agency reply (43%); DTC reported completed staff warnings/counselling in three cases.** Those corrective actions are agency-reported, not independently confirmed by the passengers; concessionaire penalties remain prospective. One headlight complaint is retained once as shared/unassigned TNSTC/SETC and excluded from both agency denominators. The median reply interval across 20 linked responses was about 5 hours 38 minutes.

## Method and limits

Counts are distinct incidents surfaced by the configured current/history handle patterns and agency search terms. Follow-ups were merged; repeated Route 252 departures on separate days were kept as separate service incidents. The joint TNSTC/SETC headlight issue was counted once and left unassigned because the post does not name an operator. Replies are counted only when visible and tied to a surfaced complaint. Dockets, apologies, referrals and promised refunds count as replies, not resolutions; DTC’s stated warnings/counselling are marked ✅ as agency-reported corrective actions, not independent passenger confirmation. Response times are approximate intervals decoded from X post IDs. This is an X-visible sample, not a count of all complaints received by agencies; deleted posts, DMs and private conversations are not visible.
