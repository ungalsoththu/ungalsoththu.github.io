## Method and limits

Counts are distinct X-visible incidents, not posts. Follow-ups are merged; agency replies are not complaints. Complaints are attributed only when the operator is named; otherwise, a shared-account case is listed once as unassigned. DTC pass details and rider identities are omitted. BMTC’s broad X retrieval gave inconsistent totals; ten complaint threads and five replies were individually paired by source post and docket, so BMTC and overall totals are conservative. Reply intervals are approximate, decoded from X post IDs. Any docket, forward, apology or promise counts as a reply, not a resolution. This is not the total number of grievances received by an agency; DMs, deleted posts and private exchanges are not visible.
