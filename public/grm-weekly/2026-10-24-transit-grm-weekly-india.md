## Method and limits

Counts represent distinct incidents surfaced in X-visible searches, not all complaints agencies received. Follow-ups were merged, including the TNSTC ticket/registration posts and the MTC 555G follow-up. Broad-name results were screened against the config inclusion/exclusion rules; praise, feature requests, official announcements, general criticism and posts without a specific service failure were excluded. A docket, inquiry, apology or explanation counts as a reply, not a resolution. Response intervals are approximate and decoded from X post IDs. Full-day date filters leave minor uncertainty around the exact 09:00 IST boundaries, particularly for zero-result searches. DMs, deleted posts and private conversations are not visible. Allegations without independent evidence are identified as unverified; passenger application numbers and other personal details are omitted.
