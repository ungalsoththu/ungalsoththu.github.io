# Daily Transit Brief — 2026-10-27 (Tuesday)

## Items

1. **[CMRL] 🚇 National award for passenger services** — CMRL won the 'Award of Excellence in Urban Transport' under "Metro Rail with the Best Passenger Services and Satisfaction" at the 19th Urban Mobility India (UMI) Conference, Bhubaneswar; presented by Union Minister Manohar Lal Khattar. Cited: ~80% cashless ticketing, leading NCMC adoption, MTC bus integration, Pink Squad. — Live Chennai, Oct 26. https://www.livechennai.com/detailnews.asp?catid=&newsid=84109

2. **[SR] 🚉 MRTS handover / CUMTA upgrade plan** — Ahead of CMRL takeover of the Beach–St. Thomas Mount MRTS, CUMTA has prepared an upgrade plan: repair of non-functional lifts/escalators, 60% of station space refurbished for passengers, remainder explored for commercial use. MoU expected shortly (Nov-end per news reports); Southern Railway to operate trains ~2 more years; metro-standard facilities ~3 years. Context: safety concerns after an October death inside Thirumayilai station. — Citizen Matters, Oct 26. https://citizenmatters.in/beyond-ghost-stations-and-unsafe-infrastructure-how-can-we-reclaim-chennais-mrts

3. **[MTC] 🚌 OMR bus shelter roofs holed** — Shelters at Kandanchavadi, Jain College, Thoraipakkam, Perungudi and IGP along Rajiv Gandhi Salai leak/roofless; commuters wait with umbrellas in rain. TNRDC: temporary repairs now; new shelters only after metro works end. — DT Next, Oct 26. https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/chennai-damaged-bus-shelter-roofs-in-omr-leave-commuters-at-natures-mercy-2

4. **[MTC] 🚌 Vadapalani bus terminus sewage stagnation** — Sewage from storm-water-drain works pooling in rainwater at the Vadapalani bus stand on Arcot Road; MTC commuters wading through it to board. GCC: the 12-km arterial will be relaid only in Jan 2027; metro + SWD works ongoing. — DT Next, Oct 27. https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/arcot-road-bruised-by-swd-construction-slammed-by-rain

5. **[SR] 🚉 Nilgiri Mountain Railway shut till Oct 29** — Restoration work extended after hill-section damage; terrain slowing repairs. — Live Chennai, Oct 26. https://www.livechennai.com/detailnews.asp?catid=&newsid=84134
