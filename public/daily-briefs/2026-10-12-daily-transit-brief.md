# Daily Transit Brief — 2026-10-12 (Mon)

Posted as @ungalsoththu. Run: 2026-10-12 09:00 IST window (covers Oct 11 ~09:00 → Oct 12 ~09:00).

## Items

1. **[CMRL] Phase 2's first stretch inaugurated.** PM Modi + TN CM C Joseph Vijay flagged off the 15-km, 11-station Poonamallee Bypass–Vadapalani stretch (Corridor 4 / Yellow Line), built at ₹4,900 crore; 3.5-km double-decker viaduct; first platform screen doors on an elevated stretch; services 4–9 PM on day 1, 7 AM–9 PM regular from Oct 12; network grows to ~69 km. — New Indian Express (C Shivakumar), Oct 11, 2026. https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Oct/11/pm-modi-inaugurates-poonamallee-vadapalani-stretch-in-chennai-takes-metro-ride-with-cm-vijay (corroborated: Times of India, Oct 11; Economic Times, Oct 11; Metro Rail News, Oct 11)

2. **[CMRL] Open, not complete.** Six stations between Porur and Vadapalani plus Vadapalani's own entry/exit pending; civil work targeted by December, then statutory approvals, fire licences and a second CRS inspection; meanwhile a temporary link bridge connects to Phase 1. — New Indian Express, Oct 11, 2026 (same URL as item 1).

3. **[CMRL] Day-1 riders: packed 3-coach trains.** No ladies coach; Pink Squad escort only until Porur. Time savings real: ~1 hr bus rides down to 25–30 min; ₹30 shared auto vs ₹30 metro fare. — New Indian Express, Oct 12, 2026. https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Oct/12/chennai-metro-yellow-line-instant-hit-but-just-3-coaches-in-each-train-a-concern

4. **[CMRL] Next milestone.** Trial runs Koyambedu–Chennai Trade Centre targeted end-November; Corridor 5 plan widened from 26 to 29.6 km (Koyambedu–Alandur via Butt Road, ~2 more months of work). Phase 1 logged 1 crore riders/month for 3 consecutive months. — Metro Rail News, Oct 11, 2026. https://metrorailnews.in/chennai-metro-phase-2-corridor-4-inaugurated-modi (Phase 1 ridership: TNIE Oct 11)

5. **[CMRL] Last-mile gap.** Tender for feeder connectivity at 41 Phase 1 stations about to be awarded; no equivalent tender yet for Phase 2 (MTC running interim feeders near the 11 new stations per The Hindu, Oct 10). — New Indian Express, Oct 11, 2026 (same URL as item 1).

6. **[SR] Reported:** PM dedicated the 4.3-km Chennai Beach–Chennai Egmore 4th line to the nation and inaugurated redeveloped Amrit Bharat stations across TN. — Southern Railway / ANI-UNI agency posts, Oct 11, 2026. https://www.facebook.com/UNINewsagency/videos/watch-chennai-tamil-nadu-prime-minister-narendra-modi-inaugurates-the-poonamalle/2158817904982007 (snippet-level; also The Hindu preview: Chennai Beach–Attipattu 4th line, 22.1 km, ₹293+ crore)
