5. **[CMRL]** 1 crore+ passengers in September 2026, after 1.04 crore in August. — Dainik Jagran English, Oct 7 — https://english.dainikjagranmpcg.com/national/pm-modi-to-inaugurate-chennai-metro-phase-ii-stretch-on/article-36634
6. **[CUMTA]** DPR tender floated Oct 7 (CUMTA/12408/UMC/2026-27-Phase-II) for 3 Model Urban Mobility Corridors: GST Road, Taramani Link Road, Velachery–Tambaram Rd (Marmalong Bridge–Walajah Rd).
