## Items (last 24h, verified)

1. **[MTC] Fleet age halved in 3 years via RTI** — Average fleet age fell 9.61 yrs (2023-24) → 4.98 yrs (Aug 2026); 1,347 buses inducted in 2025-26 (fleet 3,407 → 4,060; 4,153 across 35 depots). Yet 611 of 4,153 buses are 10+ years, past the 9-year/12-lakh-km replacement norm (TN govt, July 2021). World Bank Phase II: 750 AC e-buses on GCC, 130 launched; tender floated for 1,000 more. Kilambakkam youngest depot (1.82 yrs); Mandaveli oldest (9.16 yrs). — DT Next (G Jagannath), Oct 5: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/mtc-fleet-gets-younger-but-611-buses-are-past-retirement

2. **[TN] Madras HC disposes of plea against Vetri Payanam free-travel scheme** — Writ by Erode private bus operator N. Kalamani withdrawn after CJ Dharmadhikari + Justice Arul Murugan questioned how a 2-route operator could challenge a State-wide scheme; liberty to pursue other remedies. Scheme scale: 84.32 lakh women/day, no-fare travel on 12,695 govt buses. — The Hindu, Oct 5: https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/madras-high-court-dismisses-as-withdrawn-a-plea-to-declare-vettri-payanam-scheme-unconstitutional/article71546150.ece

3. **[CMRL] Driverless metro target mid-2027** — 15 UTO trains, 10-min frequency, 80 kmph top / 32 kmph avg, 3-car ~900-passenger sets; 120 test cases before CMRS certification. Fares: Vadapalani–Poonamallee Bypass max ₹40; longer journeys up to ₹50. — TOI, Oct 5: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/chennai-metro-goes-driverless-15-trains-10-minute-frequency-900-passenger-capacity-and-80kmph-speed-heres-what-changes-as-cmrl-targets-2027/photostory/134700463.cms

4. **[CMRL] PM Modi to inaugurate Phase II first stretch Oct 11 (Reported)** — 14.6-km Poonamallee–Vadapalani; PM to ride Poonamallee→Porur per Union Minister Pon Radhakrishnan (PTI); CMRL official confirmation awaited. — PTI/ET, Oct 5: https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/transportation/railways/pm-modi-to-inaugurate-metro-rail-project-in-chennai-on-oct-11-pon-radhakrishnan/articleshow/134705926.cms

5. **[SR] EMU wheel derailment near Fort station** — Tambaram→Beach EMU, one wheel set derailed Mon ~10:45am; Beach-bound services hit, Park/Egmore trains turned back; ART re-railed by 12:15pm; no injuries; parallel line ran. — The Hindu, Oct 5: https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/chennai/suburban-train-derails-near-fort-railway-station-chennai-beach-bound-services-hit/article71546251.ece
