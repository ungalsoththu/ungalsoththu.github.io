## Items

1. **[TN] 🛺 Auto fare revision** — Fares unchanged since 2013: ₹60 base for first 1.5 km + ₹30/km. Transport commissionerate convened a Sept 23 meeting to record consumer organisations' representations (after July 9 consultation under transport minister Vijay Tamilan Parthiban where consumers say they weren't heard); ~3 lakh auto drivers plan Sept 26 rally demanding a hike; T Sadagopan (TN Progressive Consumer Centre) counter-proposed ₹50 base + ₹20/km. — Times of India, Sep 19, https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/auto-fare-hike-govt-seeks-views-of-commuters-drivers-flag-delay/articleshow/134358435.cms

2. **[SR] 🚉 Station accessibility** — After complaints about access during redevelopment, Southern Railway arranged wheelchairs + porter assistance at Egmore (18 wheelchairs) and Tambaram (13) with a Divyangjan helpline; Egmore's new 18m footbridge (6 escalators, 5 lifts) due end-2026; Tambaram 8m-wide footbridge end-2026. — Urban Acres, Sep 19, https://urbanacres.in/chennai-railways-add-wheelchairs-and-porters-amid-station-redevelopment

3. **[SR] 🚉 Tambaram–Thiruvananthapuram special extended** — Weekly special extended: Wednesdays TVC→Tambaram to Oct 28, Thursdays return to Oct 29; running 6+ months; passengers asking for permanent status; Egmore–Kanyakumari weekly special also listed. — Urban Acres, Sep 19, https://urbanacres.in/tambaram-thiruvananthapuram-special-train-extended-until-october-29

4. **[TNSTC] 🚌 Oragadam crash** — TNSTC bus (Chengalpattu→Tiruvallur) hit a factory bus near Vadakkupattu/Oragadam, Kancheepuram; 25+ injured, treated at Chengalpattu GH. — New Indian Express, Sep 20, https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Sep/20/over-25-injured-as-buses-collide-near-oragadam (also TOI Sep 19)

5. **[MTC] 🚌 Driver killed, Moolakadai** — 58-yr-old MTC driver (Anna Nagar depot) killed by a wrong-side motorcycle while riding to duty; 17-yr-old student seriously injured. Road-safety/enforcement angle; no personal details shared in tweet. — Urban Acres, Sep 19, https://urbanacres.in/chennai-road-accident-kills-mtc-driver-in-moolakadai-collision
