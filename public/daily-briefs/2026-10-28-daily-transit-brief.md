## Items

1. **[TN] 🛺 Auto fares revised after 13 years** — Home (Transport-I) Dept notified in an extraordinary Gazette (Tuesday, Oct 27): minimum fare doubled ₹25 → ₹50 for the first 1.8 km; per-km ₹12 → ₹25 (+108%); night charge (11 pm–5 am) 1.5×; ~3.46 lakh autos covered; follows 2022 Madras HC order in a meter-compliance case. Advocacy angle: the notification is step one — meter compliance is the real test. — NDTV Profit, Oct 28. https://www.ndtvprofit.com/india/tamil-nadu-revises-auto-rickshaw-fares-after-13-years-what-commuters-should-know-12246265
