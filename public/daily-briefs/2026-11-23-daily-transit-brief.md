   — Deccan Chronicle, Nov 22: https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/tamil-nadu/guindy-multi-modal-transit-hub-for-completion-before-2030-2006758

4. **[CMRL] North Chennai parking crunch pushes vehicles onto footpaths** — 9 stations Wimco Nagar Depot–Thyagaraya College have combined 2,193 two-wheeler + 102 car slots vs 5,000–8,000 weekday footfall; pavement parking rampant near Tiruvottiyur, Theradi, Kaladipet, Tollgate on TH Road.
   — DT Next, Nov 22: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/parking-crunch-at-north-chennai-metro-stations-pushes-vehicles-onto-footpaths

5. **[SR] Tambaram skywalk plagued by broken escalators, garbage** — thousands use it daily; lifts on some platforms newly opened, but escalators out of service for want of spares, sewage overflow around it; upkeep outsourced to a private contractor whose contract period ended, Corporation signing a new one; CMDA to construct a skywalk connecting bus terminus and railway station (CUMTA special officer I Jayakumar).
   — DT Next, Nov 22: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/chennai-citizen-connect-tambaram-skywalk-plagued-by-broken-escalators-heaps-of-garbage

6. **[TNSTC] 8,012 special buses for Tiruvannamalai Karthigai Deepam (Nov 22–25); 1,713 from Chennai** — Kilambakkam 1,023 + Madhavaram 400 + Adyar 290; Deepam falls Nov 24.
