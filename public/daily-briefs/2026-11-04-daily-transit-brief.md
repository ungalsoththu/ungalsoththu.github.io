## Items

1. **[TNSTC]** Deepavali rush: ~6 lakh/day leave Chennai from Thu Nov 5; TNSTC/SETC daily reservation quota raised 1.35L→2L; 79,414 bookings Nov 5–7 (39,100 for Nov 6 alone); ~10 special trains announced (to Kanniyakumari, Sengottai, Ramanathapuram, Thiruvananthapuram etc.); 183 daily trains carry ~3.6 lakh; 3,400 TNSTC/SETC buses carry ~1.75 lakh/day; ~60,000/day by private omni buses.
   — New Indian Express, Nov 3, 2026: https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Nov/03/6-lakh-to-leave-chennai-per-day-for-deepavali-from-thursday

2. **[CMRL]** Peak-hour ticketing blackout: technical glitch disrupted ticketing/AFC gates across metro stations ~1 hour Tuesday evening (Nov 3); app-ticket riders stranded inside (TN Secretariat Association secretary at Vadapalani Phase I ~6:15 pm), counter printers also down; normal ops restored.
   — DT Next, Nov 3, 2026: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/technical-glitches-at-chennai-metro-stations-rankle-passengers

3. **[CMRL]** Metro Connect feeder launched (Mon Nov 2): links One Paramount corporate campus (Porur) with Porur Bypass metro station; 17+1-seater AC vehicles, app booking + live tracking; CMRL invites IT parks, SEZs, hospitals, colleges to partner.
   — News Today, Nov 3, 2026: https://newstodaynet.com/2026/11/03/cmrl-launches-metro-connect-service-linking-corporate-campus-with-porur-bypass-station
   — Also DT Next, Nov 2: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/cmrl-launches-metro-connect-service-linking-corporate-campus-with-porur-bypass-station

4. **[CMDA]** Kilambakkam skywalk misses a third deadline: ₹79-crore, 275-m skywalk linking proposed Kilambakkam railway station across GST Road with Kalaignar Centenary Bus Terminus now due at Pongal; project launched Dec 2023, terminus opened Dec 30, 2023; until it opens, Deepavali riders reach KCBT by road.
   — DT Next, Nov 4, 2026: https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/kilambakkam-skywalk-misses-another-deadline

5. **[MTC]** Transport minister A. Vijay Tamilan Parthiban's advisory meeting at MTC HQ (Nov 3): drivers/conductors told to treat passengers courteously, not drop mid-route; passenger complaints (phone/social media) to be probed by branch/regional managers with strict action; Deepavali bonus already paid; double pay for Vidial (dawn) service crews; mental-health counselling via consultants.
   — Nakkheeran (Tamil), Nov 3, 2026: https://www.nakkheeran.in/24-by-7-news/minister-vijay-thamizhan-parthiban-issues-a-warning-to-govt-bus-drivers-and-conductors-12780830
