## Items

1. **[SR]** Tambaram–Chengalpattu 4th line stalled 13+ months after approval (Oct 2025); two phases — Tambaram–Guduvancherry, Guduvancherry–Chengalpattu; tender floated in Oct, work "to start soon". Beach–Chengalpattu is SR's highest-patronage suburban section; 3rd line commissioned 2022, but SR says services beyond Tambaram can increase only after the 4th line gives suburban trains dedicated tracks (long-distance trains currently share suburban tracks). Significance up with Kilambakkam bus terminus + Tambaram being developed as Chennai's third railway terminal; Kilambakkam halt + skywalk (bus terminus ↔ station) State-funded.
