  1. [TN] 🛺 Auto fare revision — Fares unchanged since 2013: ₹60 base for first 1.5 km + ₹30/km. Transport commissionerate convened a Sept 23 meeting to record consumer organisations' representations (after July 9 consultation under transport minister Vijay Tamilan Parthiban where consumers say they weren't heard); ~3 lakh auto drivers plan Sept 26 rally demanding a hike; T Sadagopan (TN Progressive Consumer Centre) counter-proposed ₹50 base + ₹20/km. — Times of India, Sep 19, https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/auto-fare-hike-govt-seeks-views-of-commuters-drivers-flag-delay/articleshow/134358435.cms

[SR] 🚉 Tambaram–Thiruvananthapuram special extended — Weekly special extended: Wednesdays TVC→Tambaram to Oct 28, Thursdays return to Oct 29; running 6+ months; passengers asking for permanent status; Egmore–Kanyakumari weekly special also listed. — Urban Acres, Sep 19, https://urbanacres.in/tambaram-thiruvananthapuram-special-train-extended-until-october-29

[TNSTC] 🚌 Oragadam crash — TNSTC bus (Chengalpattu→Tiruvallur) hit a factory bus near Vadakkupattu/Oragadam, Kancheepuram; 25+ injured, treated at Chengalpattu GH. — New Indian Express, Sep 20, https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Sep/20/over-25-injured-as-buses-collide-near-oragadam (also TOI Sep 19)

Road-safety/enforcement angle; no personal details shared in tweet. — Urban Acres, Sep 19, https://urbanacres.in/chennai-road-accident-kills-mtc-driver-in-moolakadai-collision
