4. **[SR]** Chengalpattu–Tambaram 4th line (30.2 km) to be built in phases; phase 1 = 21.5 km Chengalpattu–Guduvanchery at ₹273 crore; total project cost expected to rise from ₹713.56 cr to ₹756 crore. The Chengalpattu–Guduvanchery section runs at 87% line capacity utilisation, projected 136% by project completion without the 4th line; 236 services/day on Chennai–Chengalpattu carry ~5 lakh daily passengers; expected +25% line capacity. — New Indian Express, Oct 10: https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/chennai/2026/Oct/10/southern-railway-to-build-21-km-fourth-line-between-chengalpattu-guduvanchery-in-first-phase

5. **[GCTP]** Traffic diversions today (Oct 11) 8 am–4:30 pm for the PM's visit — heavy/commercial vehicles diverted at GST Rd, Mount Poonamallee Rd, OMR points; MTC bus movement affected at Porur during Saturday's convoy rehearsal. — The Hindu, Oct 10: https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/chennai/pm-modis-chennai-visit-traffic-diversions-on-october-11/article71567398.ece
