## Items

1. **[TNSTC]** Deepavali operations begin today: 15,659 buses out of Chennai Nov 5–7 (9,086 specials + 6,573 regular); return rush 11,197 buses Nov 9–10 (6,815 specials). Services from 5 terminals + temporary facilities, destination-wise. MTC free feeders KCBT Kilambakkam ↔ Maraimalai Nagar temp terminus, 24x7 Koyambedu↔KCBT; 14 reservation counters (10 at KCBT, 2 Koyambedu, 2 Maraimalai Nagar); crew batta doubled; control room 94450 14436 / 149.
   — New Indian Express, Nov 5, 2026: https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/tamil-nadu/2026/Nov/05/9086-special-buses-from-chennai-to-ease-deepavali-travel-rush
   — Also DT Next, Nov 4: https://www.dtnext.in/news/tamilnadu/over-15000-special-buses-from-today-to-manage-deepavali-rush; TOI: https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chennai/15659-buses-to-operate-from-chennai-for-diwali-direct-services-from-16-locations/articleshow/135550686.cms; TN8: https://tn8.in/deepavali-2026-15659-buses-chennai-nov-5-7-16-boarding-points-tnstc

2. **[CMRL]** North Chennai residents press for two new metro corridors: Federation of North Chennai Residents' Welfare Associations petitioned CMRL MD Ashish Chatterjee — extend Madhavaram–Wimco Nagar corridor ~11 km toward Ennore/Tiruvottiyur (DPR ₹88 lakh proposed), a new Mint–Sengundram corridor, and an Ennore Multimodal Transport Hub integrating port-area services.
   — The Hindu, Nov 4, 2026: https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/chennai/north-chennai-residents-seek-two-new-metro-rail-corridors/article71664526.ece

3. **[CMRL]** Reported: >90% viaduct complete on the ~15 km Koyambedu–Alandur stretch (Phase 2 Corridor 5); CMRL targets viaduct + track laying by Dec 2026, trial runs Jan 2027, public services targeted Mar 2027. Station completion (6 stations targeted Dec–Jan) and signalling remain the open risks.
   — LiveChennai, Nov 4, 2026: https://www.livechennai.com/detailnews.asp?newsid=84444

4. **[SR]** Southern Railway adds 2 special unreserved MEMU trains (12 coaches each) Tambaram–Manamadurai for Deepavali: 06161 departs Nov 6 5.30 pm, 06159 departs Nov 7 4.35 pm; return services Nov 7 & Nov 9; stops incl. Villupuram, Trichy, Madurai.
   — LiveChennai, Nov 4, 2026: https://www.livechennai.com/detailnews.asp?newsid=84465
